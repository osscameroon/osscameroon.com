### The OSS Cameroon Spirit

More than just a group of developers, **OSS Cameroon** is a tech family. Our strength lies in our diversity and our shared commitment to advancing the Cameroonian digital ecosystem through the free sharing of knowledge.

## How We Connect & Grow

We bring the community to life through consistent activities designed to bridge the gap between theory and practice.

### 🎙️ OSS Talk
Our knowledge-sharing webinars.
- **The Concept:** Experts or enthusiasts present specific topics ranging from system architecture and security to career roadmaps.
- **The Goal:** Demystify complex concepts and spark debates on industry best practices.

### 💻 Live Coding Sessions
Nothing beats seeing code written in real-time.
- **Direct Learning:** Watch seasoned contributors build features from scratch, handle bugs, and manage workflows.
- **Interactive:** Ask questions in the chat while the developer explains their logic line by line.

### 📖 Dev Stories
Every developer has a unique journey. **Dev Stories** highlight our members' paths.
- **Experience Sharing:** Failures, successes, career pivots, and advice for the next generation.
- **Inspiration:** Humanizing tech to show that behind every repository is a personal adventure.

### 🏆 Hackathons & Code Sprints
Intensive sessions to transform ideas into functional prototypes.
- **Hackathons:** Friendly competitions to solve societal problems via code.
- **Code Sprints:** Focused days dedicated to pushing major updates for our core projects like Jobsika or CamerApps.

---

## Open Source Contribution

Contribution is the heartbeat of OSS Cameroon. We make it easy for everyone to get involved:

1.  **Code & Design:** Fix bugs, add features, or improve UI/UX on our [GitHub](https://github.com/osscameroon).
2.  **Ideation:** Use the [Project Ideas](https://github.com/osscameroon/project-ideas) repository to propose tech solutions for local challenges in transport, health, or education.
3.  **Mentorship:** Experienced members provide Code Reviews, helping newcomers improve their skills while contributing to real-world tools.

---

## Values & Inclusion

To ensure every member feels safe and respected, we strictly follow our **Code of Conduct**. We believe in:
- **Respect and Courtesy:** Professionalism in all interactions.
- **Inclusivity:** All skill levels—from "Hello World" to Senior Architect—are welcome.
- **Constructive Feedback:** We grow by helping each other improve.

> **Join the movement.** Whether you want to watch a Live Coding session, share your Dev Story, or start your first contribution, there is a place for you here.
