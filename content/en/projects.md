## Software & Platforms
*Open-source tools built to solve local challenges and empower developers.*

**Jobsika**  
A community-driven platform for company reviews and salary transparency in Cameroon. It empowers job seekers with real data to negotiate better terms and identify healthy work environments.
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/osscameroon/jobsika)

**Lewa**  
A modern and lightweight URL shortener service built by the community. It focuses on simplicity and efficiency for sharing links within the ecosystem.
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/osscameroon/lewa)

**Place**  
A project dedicated to mapping and location-based services, aiming to provide open-source alternatives for discovering places and navigating the local context.
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/osscameroon/place)

**Project Ideas (The Incubator)**  
Our "Think Tank" for brainstorming tech solutions. This is where every major project starts, allowing anyone to propose a solution for transport, health, or education before code is written.
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/osscameroon/project-ideas)

**CamerApps**  
A curated directory of applications and digital communities made in Cameroon. It increases visibility for local creators and centralizes the "Made in Cameroon" tech scene.
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/osscameroon/camerapps)

**OSS Cameroon Website**  
The digital face of our organization. Built using Hugo and the CNCF Dot Org theme, it serves as a primary "sandbox" for new members to learn Git workflows and static site generation.
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/osscameroon/osscameroon-website)

**MiniYotas**  
Our internal gamification and reward system that tracks contributions and honors members for their work, fostering a culture of appreciation.

## Community & Knowledge Sharing
*Events and programs designed to foster professional growth and inspiration.*

**OSS Talk**  
Our technical webinar series where experts share production-level insights. These sessions bridge the gap between academic theory and industry reality.

**Live Coding Sessions**  
Real-time, interactive development workshops where we build features live. This is a key way for beginners to observe professional problem-solving and debugging.

**Dev Stories**  
A storytelling initiative that highlights the personal journeys of our members. We share these stories to humanize tech and inspire the next generation.

**The Technical Blog**  
A multilingual hub for tutorials and community updates. It allows members to build their technical authority while documenting the community's growth.

**Community Hackathons**  
High-energy events focused on prototyping solutions for social problems. These events turn ideas into functional tools through intense collaboration.
