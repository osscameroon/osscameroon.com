Supporter Membership is designed for organizations — startups, agencies, and enterprises — that want to make a meaningful and visible commitment to the Cameroonian developer ecosystem.

Your monthly contribution directly funds:

- Developer grants
- Community infrastructure
- Hosting and operational costs
- Events, hackathons, and mentorship programs

## Membership Benefits

Supporter Members receive:

- Premium logo placement on the OSS Cameroon homepage and Members page
- Dedicated recognition across our social platforms, including Twitter, Telegram, and GitHub
- Priority visibility on the community job board
- Opportunities to sponsor or co-host OSS Cameroon events and hackathons
- Direct access to the OSS Cameroon team for partnerships and collaboration

## Community Impact

Supporter organizations help strengthen the local open source ecosystem and empower the next generation of African developers.

Current and future supporters may include startups, telecom providers, educational organizations, and global technology companies aligned with our mission.
