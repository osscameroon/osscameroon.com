{{< intro >}}
An open source society driven by Cameroonian developers, building solutions for Africa one commit at a time.
{{< /intro >}}


OSS Cameroon is a community of developers, designers, and tech enthusiasts united by one mission: building the open source solutions Cameroon needs.

Founded in Douala, we believe Africa’s challenges deserve African-built solutions — created openly, shared freely, and maintained collaboratively.

Our country is rapidly growing its tech ecosystem, and we are here to accelerate that growth. We mentor junior developers, fund those who lack access to equipment or internet, foster team collaboration, and ship real products that solve real problems in our society.

Some of our community-driven initiatives include:

- [**Jobsika**](https://jobsika.cm) — improving salary transparency for job seekers
- [**Lewa**](https://lewa.osscameroon.com) — supporting African writing systems and languages
- [**Place**](https://place.osscameroon.com) — helping people discover local events and communities

Today, OSS Cameroon represents a growing movement of builders across the country:

- 55+ open source repositories
- 41 active contributors
- A vibrant community across GitHub, Twitter, and Telegram

OSS Cameroon is proof that world-class software can be built right here in Cameroon.
