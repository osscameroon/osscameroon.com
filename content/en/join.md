{{< intro >}}
OSS Cameroon is more than just a code repository; it is a movement. We are a community of Cameroonian developers created in January 2021 that aims to learn, share skills, and build impactful open-source solutions for our society.
{{< /intro >}}


## Membership Types

We offer different ways to be part of our ecosystem, depending on your goals and background:

### 1. Individuals (Community Members)
For developers, designers, and tech enthusiasts who want to contribute their skills or learn from others.
- **Benefits:** Access to our Telegram community (450+ members), mentorship opportunities, and the ability to contribute to official projects.
- **Ideal for:** Students, professional developers, and hobbyists.
- **How to join:** [Join our Telegram Group](https://t.me/joinchat/UpKZh_T3W02LsGvQ) and introduce yourself!

Learn more about becoming an individual member on our [Individuals](/about/individuals/) page.

### 2. Associates
For existing projects, local tech communities, and foundations that share our vision and want to collaborate.
- **Goal:** To create a network of tech hubs across Cameroon (Python Cameroun, Laravel Cameroun, Flutter Cameroun, etc.) to popularize tech culture.

Learn more about becoming an associate member on our [Associates](/about/associates/) page.

### 3. Sponsors
For organizations and companies worldwide that want to support the Cameroonian tech ecosystem.
- **Goal:** Providing resources, infrastructure, or funding to help us organize meetups, hackathons, and maintain our digital infrastructure.

Learn more about becoming a sponsor on our [Sponsors](/about/sponsors/) page.

## How to Get Started

Getting started with OSS Cameroon is simple and follows these three steps:

1.  **Join the Conversation:** Connect with us on [Telegram](https://t.me/joinchat/UpKZh_T3W02LsGvQ) or follow us on [Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/osscameroon). This is where we discuss everything from coding dojos to industry trends.
2.  **Explore our Projects:** Visit our [GitHub Organization](https://github.com/osscameroon) to see what we are building.
3.  **Read our Blog:** Stay updated with the latest community news and technical tutorials at [blog.osscameroon.com](https://blog.osscameroon.com).

## How to Contribute to Projects

You don't need to be a "pro" to contribute. We welcome code, documentation, design, and project ideas.

### 1. Improve Existing Projects
- **Find an Issue:** Visit our [GitHub Organization](https://github.com/osscameroon) and look for issues labeled `good first issue` in repositories like **Jobsika** or **CamerApps**.
- **The Workflow:** Fork the repo, create a branch, make your changes, and submit a Pull Request (PR).

### 2. Propose a New Project (Project Ideas)
If you have an idea for a tool or platform that solves a problem in the Cameroonian context, don't keep it to yourself! We have a dedicated space for this:

- **The [Project Ideas Repository](https://github.com/osscameroon/project-ideas):** This is our "incubator." 
- **How it works:** Open an **Issue** in the `project-ideas` repo using the provided template. Describe the problem, your proposed solution, and the tech stack you're thinking of.
- **Community Feedback:** The community will discuss the idea with you. If there is enough interest and the project aligns with our mission, we will create a formal repository for it under the OSS Cameroon organization.

## The "Getting Started" Checklist

1.  **Join the Chat:** Hop into our [Telegram Group](https://t.me/joinchat/UpKZh_T3W02LsGvQ).
2.  **Browse the Ideas:** Check the [Project Ideas Issue board](https://github.com/osscameroon/project-ideas/issues) to see what others are dreaming up—you might find a project you want to co-found!
3.  **Claim a Task:** If you prefer starting small, pick a `good first issue` on an active repo.
4.  **Attend an Event:** Keep an eye out for our "Open Source Weekends" or virtual workshops.

### Our Core Projects:
- **Jobsika:** A platform for company ratings and salaries in Cameroon.
- **CamerApps:** A directory of useful Cameroonian applications and communities.
- **MiniYotas:** Contribute to projects and earn "Yotas" (our community reward system).
- **OSS Cameroon Website:** Help us improve the very site you are browsing.
