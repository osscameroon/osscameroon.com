For developers and tech enthusiasts who want to support OSS Cameroon personally.

Becoming an Individual Member means you directly fuel the work of Cameroonian developers.

Your annual contribution helps fund:

- Developer equipment
- Internet access
- Hosting and infrastructure costs
- Community events and workshops

## Membership Benefits

As an Individual Member, you receive:

- Your name and profile featured on the Members page of `osscameroon.com`
- Early access to community events, hackathons, and workshops
- Access to a private Members Telegram channel
- An OSS Cameroon digital badge for GitHub or LinkedIn
- The opportunity to directly support Cameroon’s growing open source ecosystem
