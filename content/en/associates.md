OSS Cameroon believes the open source ecosystem grows stronger when communities support one another.

Associate Membership is available at no cost to:

- Registered non-profit organizations
- Open source foundations
- Developer and tech communities
- Mission-aligned educational or civic initiatives

## Membership Benefits

Associate Members receive:

- Organization logo and website link featured on the Members page
- Cross-promotion opportunities for projects and events
- Access to our contributor network for collaboration and mentorship
- Participation in annual roadmap discussions and community initiatives

## Eligibility

Applicants must demonstrate non-profit status or provide evidence of mission alignment with OSS Cameroon.

OSS Cameroon reserves the right to review and approve all Associate Member applications.
